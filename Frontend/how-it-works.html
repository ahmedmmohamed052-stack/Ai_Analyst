What you don't see

The 18-stage AI engine

Your dataset gets queried, quality-checked, statistically analyzed, correlated, trended, and audited for outliers — with an LLM interpreting the meaning of every result along the way — before anything is written to your report.

18
chained AI stages
1
plain-English question in
1
decision-ready report out
01
Intent
Business Question Chain
The LLM reads your plain-English question and figures out what you're actually asking for — the real business intent behind the words.
02
Query
SQL Generation Chain
That intent is translated into an actual database query, written against your dataset's real schema.
SELECT ... FROM sales WHERE quarter = 'Q3' …
03
Safety
SQL Validation
Before anything touches your data, the generated query is checked for correctness and safety — no destructive statements ever run.
04
Execution
Database Execution
The validated query actually runs against your uploaded file or connected database, pulling the real rows needed to answer you.
05
Quality
Data Quality Calculation
The returned data is scored for completeness, nulls, duplicates and consistency before any conclusions are drawn from it.
06
Quality
Data Quality Interpretation
The LLM explains, in plain language, what those quality scores actually mean for how much to trust the answer.
07
Statistics
Statistical Calculation
Core statistics — averages, distributions, variance and more — are computed across the relevant fields.
08
Statistics
Statistical Interpretation
The raw numbers are turned into a narrative — what's normal, what's not, and why it matters to the business.
09
Relationships
Correlation Calculation
The engine looks for relationships between variables — which metrics move together, and how strongly.
10
Relationships
Correlation Interpretation
Those relationships are explained in business terms — is this a meaningful driver, or just noise?
11
Trends
Trend Calculation
Time-series movement is measured — is the metric you asked about rising, falling, or flat, and how fast.
12
Trends
Trend Interpretation
The trend is narrated — what changed, over what period, and how that lines up with your original question.
13
Anomalies
Outlier Calculation
Statistically unusual points — spikes, drops, one-off events — are detected across the dataset.
14
Anomalies
Outlier Interpretation
Each anomaly is explained — is it a data error, a one-time event, or a real signal worth flagging?
15
Synthesis
Exploratory Data Analysis Chain
Every calculation and interpretation so far — quality, stats, correlation, trend, outliers — is pulled together into one coherent picture of the data.
16
Reasoning
Root Cause Chain
The LLM reasons about *why* the pattern happened — not just what happened, but the likely underlying cause.
17
Reasoning
Insight Chain
The most important, non-obvious takeaways are distilled out of everything the pipeline has learned so far.
18
Action
Recommendation Chain
Concrete, actionable next steps are generated — the "so what do I actually do about it" answer to your original question.
✦
Delivery
Final Report Assembled
All 18 stages are compiled into one structured report — summary, data quality, statistics, trends, root causes, insights and recommendations — ready to render on your screen or export as a PDF.
